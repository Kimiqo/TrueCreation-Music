# TrueCreation — MVP overview

Listen closer. Understand more.

TrueCreation is a browser workspace for understanding how music is made. A creator uploads a track, explores separated stems, examines musical estimates, investigates a passage and exports audio for further work.

## The opportunity
Stem separation opens the track. TrueCreation's intended differentiation is the connected workflow after separation: listening, musical analysis, sound investigation and eventually reference comparisons supported by evidence.

## Working today
- WAV/MP3 upload, up to 3 minutes / 75 MB.
- Six main stems with actual waveforms, synchronized playback, mute, solo and volume.
- Live tempo (50–150%) and transpose (±12 semitones).
- Timestamped estimated chord progression; estimated tempo/key where available.
- Optional six-component drum separation in local evaluation.
- Passage level/spectral analysis and saved investigations.
- Original stem downloads and separately prepared tempo/pitch exports.

## Current stage
A functioning local, single-user MVP with a recorded working demonstration. No public service, commercial deployment, user traction, revenue or financial forecasts are claimed by this overview.

## Next milestones
1. Secure invited-user hosting: authentication, isolated storage and jobs, usage bounds, retention and monitoring.
2. Commercial model-license resolution, quality evaluation and hosted processing benchmarks.
3. Creator pilots to test workflow value and processing economics.
4. A permissioned reference catalog and validated comparison evidence.

## Product boundaries
Reference matching and sample-source identification are not available. Musical estimates may be wrong. Piano/guitar separation is experimental and may contain leakage. Optional drum checkpoint rights remain unresolved for commercial use. Original instruments, presets and production tools cannot be reliably identified from arbitrary tracks by this MVP.
